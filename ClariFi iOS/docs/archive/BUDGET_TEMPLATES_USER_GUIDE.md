# ClariFi Budget Templates - User Guide

## Overview
ClariFi now offers 18 professionally designed budget templates to help you get started with financial planning. Each template is tailored to specific life situations and financial goals.

## How to Use Templates

1. Open ClariFi and navigate to the Budget tab
2. Tap "Create New Budget"
3. Browse through the available templates
4. Select the template that best matches your situation
5. Enter your total monthly income (optional - templates work with or without this)
6. Review and customize the suggested categories
7. Save your budget

## Template Features

### All Templates Include:
- ✅ Pre-configured categories with suggested amounts
- ✅ Percentage-based allocations (scales with your income)
- ✅ Alert thresholds to warn when approaching limits
- ✅ Color-coded categories for easy visualization
- ✅ Recommended budget period (weekly or monthly)
- ✅ Rollover settings for unused amounts

### Customization Options:
- Add or remove categories
- Adjust amounts for any category
- Change alert thresholds
- Enable/disable rollover per category
- Rename categories to match your needs
- Set custom budget periods

## Tips for Success

1. **Start with a Template**: Even if it's not perfect, it's easier to modify than starting from scratch
2. **Track for a Month**: Use the template as-is for one month to see how it fits
3. **Adjust as Needed**: Customize categories based on your actual spending patterns
4. **Review Regularly**: Update your budget as your life situation changes
5. **Use Multiple Templates**: Create different budgets for different scenarios

## Percentage Allocations

All templates follow the general guideline:
- **Housing**: 25-32% (varies by template)
- **Savings/Debt**: 10-40% (higher for debt payoff/savings goals)
- **Food**: 10-15%
- **Transportation**: 5-10%
- **Other Essentials**: 15-25%
- **Discretionary**: 5-15%

## Switching Templates

You can create multiple budgets and switch between them:
1. Create a new budget with a different template
2. Your old budget is saved but deactivated
3. Only one budget can be active at a time
4. Switch back anytime from the Budget settings

## Need Help?

- **Can't find a template?** Try the one closest to your situation and customize it
- **Multiple situations?** Choose the template that matches your primary financial goal
- **Unique circumstances?** Start with "Professional" or "Minimalist" and heavily customize
