## Template Selection Guide

### Choose **Student Budget** if you:
- Are in college or university
- Have tuition and book expenses
- Live on a tight budget
- Need to track educational expenses

### Choose **Gig Worker Budget** if you:
- Are a freelancer or contractor
- Have variable income
- Need to set aside money for taxes
- Have business expenses

### Choose **Family Budget** if you:
- Have dependent children
- Manage household expenses
- Need childcare budgeting
- Balance multiple family needs

### Choose **Professional Budget** if you:
- Have stable full-time employment
- Focus on career growth
- Want balanced lifestyle spending
- Prioritize savings and investments

### Choose **Retiree Budget** if you:
- Are retired or semi-retired
- Live on fixed income
- Have significant healthcare costs
- Focus on essential expenses

### Choose **Single Parent Budget** if you:
- Are a single parent
- Balance work and childcare
- Need emergency fund focus
- Manage household solo

### Choose **Young Professional Budget** if you:
- Are early in your career
- Have student loans
- Building emergency fund
- Focus on career development

### Choose **Minimalist Budget** if you:
- Practice minimalism
- Pursue financial independence (FIRE)
- Want maximum savings rate
- Focus on essentials only

### Choose **Entrepreneur Budget** if you:
- Own a small business
- Mix business and personal expenses
- Need tax planning
- Invest in business growth

### Choose **Couple Budget** if you:
- Manage shared finances
- Are married or living together
- No children (yet)
- Balance lifestyle and savings

## Customization Tips

All templates are fully customizable. After selecting a template:

1. **Adjust Percentages**: Modify any category allocation
2. **Add Categories**: Create custom categories for your needs
3. **Remove Categories**: Delete categories you don't need
4. **Set Alerts**: Adjust alert thresholds per category
5. **Change Period**: Switch between weekly and monthly
6. **Enable Rollover**: Turn on/off unused amount rollover

## Template Philosophy

Each template is designed with:
- **Realistic Allocations**: Based on financial planning best practices
- **Flexibility**: Easy to customize after selection
- **Education**: Shows recommended spending patterns
- **Balance**: Considers needs, wants, and savings
- **Practicality**: Tested against real-world budgets

## Getting Started

1. Open ClariFi app
2. Navigate to Budget Creation
3. Browse available templates
4. Select the template that best matches your situation
5. Review and customize categories
6. Set your total budget amount (optional)
7. Create your budget and start tracking!

---

**Note**: Templates are starting points. Your actual budget should reflect your unique financial situation, goals, and priorities. Don't hesitate to customize!
