# How to Change Your Currency in ClariFi

## Quick Guide

Want to change from USD to CAD (or any other currency)? Here's how:

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Open ClariFi** 📱
   - Launch the app on your device

2. **Go to Planning Tab** 🎯
   - Tap the "Planning" tab at the bottom of the screen
   - (It's the third tab with a slider icon)

3. **Find Currency Settings** 💰
   - Scroll to the "App Settings" section
   - Look for "Currency" (it's the first option)
   - You'll see your current currency code (e.g., "USD")

4. **Tap Currency** 👆
   - Tap on the "Currency" row
   - This opens the Currency Settings screen

5. **Select Your Currency** 🇨🇦
   - **Option A**: Use the search bar
     - Type "CAD" or "Canadian"
     - Tap on "🇨🇦 CAD - Canadian Dollar"
   
   - **Option B**: Scroll through the list
     - Browse all 15 available currencies
     - Each shows: Flag, Code, Name, and Example amount
     - Tap your preferred currency

6. **Confirm** ✅
   - You'll see a confirmation message
   - "Your currency has been changed to Canadian Dollar"
   - Tap "OK"

7. **Done!** 🎉
   - All amounts now display in your selected currency
   - New transactions will use this currency
   - Your preference is saved automatically

## What Changes When You Switch Currency?

### ✅ What Updates Automatically
- All transaction amounts display in new currency
- Budget amounts show in new currency
- Account balances use new currency
- Charts and graphs update
- All new transactions use new currency

### ℹ️ What Stays the Same
- Your existing transaction data (amounts don't convert)
- Your budget allocations (amounts don't convert)
- Your account balances (amounts don't convert)

**Note**: The app doesn't convert amounts between currencies. If you had $100 USD and switch to CAD, it will show as CA$100 (not converted to the equivalent CAD amount). This is intentional - you're changing the display currency, not converting your money.

## Tips

### 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Choose Your Currency Early**
   - Set your preferred currency when you first set up the app
   - This ensures all your data is in the right currency from the start

2. **Stick with One Currency**
   - For accurate tracking, use one currency consistently
   - Only switch if you've moved to a new country or changed your primary currency

3. **Use Search**
   - The search bar makes finding your currency quick
   - Type the currency code (CAD) or name (Canadian)

4. **Check the Example**
   - Each currency shows an example amount (e.g., CA$100.00)
   - This helps you see how amounts will be formatted

### ⚠️ Important Notes

1. **No Automatic Conversion**
   - Switching currency doesn't convert your amounts
   - $100 USD becomes CA$100, not CA$135 (the converted amount)
   - This is by design - you're changing display format, not converting money

2. **Existing Data**
   - Your existing transactions keep their amounts
   - Only the currency symbol changes
   - This is intentional for data integrity

3. **New Transactions**
   - All new transactions will use your selected currency
   - The currency is saved with each transaction

## Troubleshooting

### Currency Not Changing?

**Problem**: Selected a new currency but amounts still show old currency  
**Solution**: 
- Force close the app and reopen
- Check that you tapped "Done" after selecting
- Verify in Settings that currency changed

### Can't Find My Currency?

**Problem**: Your currency isn't in the list  
**Solution**: 
- Currently supports 15 major currencies
- More currencies coming in future updates
- Use the closest major currency for now

### Amounts Look Wrong?

**Problem**: Amounts have wrong number of decimals  
**Solution**: 
- Some currencies (JPY, KRW) don't use decimals
- This is correct - these currencies don't have cents
- Other currencies use 2 decimal places

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Will my amounts be converted to the new currency?
**A**: No, only the currency symbol changes. $100 USD becomes CA$100, not the converted equivalent.

### Q: Can I have different currencies for different accounts?
**A**: Not yet. Currently, one currency is used throughout the app. Multi-currency accounts are planned for a future update.

### Q: What happens to my old transactions?
**A**: They keep their amounts but display with the new currency symbol.

### Q: Can I switch back to my old currency?
**A**: Yes! You can change your currency anytime. Just follow the same steps.

### Q: Does this work offline?
**A**: Yes! Currency selection works completely offline. No internet required.

### Q: Will this affect my budgets?
**A**: The amounts stay the same, only the currency symbol changes.

## Technical Details

### How Currency Formatting Works

ClariFi uses a sophisticated actor-based caching system for currency formatting:

**Thread-Safe Caching:**
- Currency formatters are cached in a `FormatterCache` actor
- Actor isolation ensures thread-safe access from any thread
- Formatters are reused for performance

**Sync and Async APIs:**
- Synchronous methods for Views and computed properties
- Async methods for background processing
- Both use the same cached formatters

**Example:**
```swift
// Synchronous (most common)
let formatted = CurrencyFormatter.shared.format(100.50, currency: .cad)
// Result: "CA$100.50"

// Async (in async contexts)
let formatted = await CurrencyFormatter.shared.formatAsync(100.50, currency: .cad)
// Result: "CA$100.50"
```
